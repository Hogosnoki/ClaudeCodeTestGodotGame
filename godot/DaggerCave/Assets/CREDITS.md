# Asset credits

Every file under `Assets/` comes from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and is released under
[CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain, no attribution
required; credited here anyway). Maps were downloaded at 1K and re-encoded as JPEG.

## Textures (`Textures/<name>/<name>_{diff,nor,arm}.jpg`)

`diff` is the albedo, `nor` the OpenGL-convention normal map, `arm` packs ambient occlusion (R),
roughness (G) and metalness (B).
